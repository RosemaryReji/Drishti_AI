# Drishti AI Design System & UX Design Document

## Project
Drishti AI — India's Climate Digital Twin

## Design Vision

"Create a living digital mirror of India's climate that feels scientifically trustworthy, visually immersive, and accessible to every citizen."

---

# Design Philosophy

Drishti AI combines two visual worlds:

1. Scientific GIS & Remote Sensing Visualization
2. Futuristic Climate Digital Twin Experiences

The product should feel like:

- NASA Mission Control
- Windy.com
- ArcGIS Digital Twin
- Iron Man's JARVIS Interface
- Digital India Infrastructure

Core principle:

Transform complex climate science into an immersive experience understandable within seconds.
